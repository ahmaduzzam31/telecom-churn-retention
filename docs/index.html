  • Lock in new customers early. 55% of month-to-month customers leave within 6 months, against about 10% on 1-year contracts.
  • Fibre has a service problem. It churns more than DSL at every tenure. Fibre without tech support churns at 49%.
  • Payment friction matters. Electronic check payers churn at 45%. Moving them to automatic payment is a quick win.
  • Target, don't blanket. Contacting everyone loses money, while model-based targeting is profitable.
